Hi there,

My name is Gonzalo Oyanedel Vial. I'm a Chilean biochemist, engineer and computer scientist.

I'm currently Head of Artificial Intelligence at Walmart Chile, where I lead Data Science, Machine Learning and AI Acceleration for the market.

I also serve on the boards of [Teatro Municipal de Las Condes](https://tmlascondes.cl/), [Huna](https://drinkhuna.com/) and [Mar Futura](https://www.marfutura.org/), and on the Board of Advisors at [Skyward](https://skyward.ai/).

I occasionally teach Data Science at the [Facultad de Ingeniería y Ciencias, Universidad Diego Portales](https://ingenieriayciencias.udp.cl/).

I write at [Los dados no juegan a Dios](https://gonzaloo.substack.com/), and you can connect with me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/gonzalo-oyanedel-vial/).
